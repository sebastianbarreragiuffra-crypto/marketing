## Prompt final

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: one photographic triptych contact sheet used as three adjacent illustrative campaign thumbnails inside an HTML marketing dashboard. The supplied image is a visual style reference only, not an edit target. Create one wide rectangular image with three equal-width adjoining vertical panels, edge to edge, no gutters. Left panel: stylish cozy living room with a warm mustard sofa, modern floor lamp, plants and wood furniture, warm interior photography. Middle panel: single unbranded dark navy athletic sneaker on a pale pedestal, dramatic teal blue flowing fabric backdrop, premium product photograph. Right panel: beautiful modern beige kitchen with warm timber, marble countertop, green plants and natural daylight, architectural interior photograph. Match the tasteful photographic mood of the three small campaign creatives in the supplied screenshot. Each subject is centered within its third and has usable quiet negative space at top center for HTML copy. No text whatsoever, no logos, no people, no UI, no buttons, no watermarks. This is one continuous triptych with exactly three equal-width panels, width-to-height about 3:1.
